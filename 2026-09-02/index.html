JavisDiT++: Unified Modeling and Optimization for Joint Audio-Video Generation

Kai Liu, Yanhao Zheng, Kai Wang, Shengqiong Wu, Rongjunchen Zhang, Jiebo Luo, Dimitrios Hatzinakos, Ziwei Liu, Hao Fei, Tat-Seng Chua

ICLR 2026 poster · Native text-to-audio-video generation built on Wan2.1-1.3B-T2V.

ICLR 2026arXiv:2602.19163JAVGWan2.1 backbone
Source: cover p.1; Abstract pp.1-2; project page listed in paper
TL;DR

A 2.1B open JAVG model beats larger prior systems by making time shared, modalities separated, and preference pairs modality-aware.

The interesting part is not one module; it is the decomposition: attention exchanges audio-video information, FFNs preserve modality-specific detail, RoPE locks time, and AV-DPO cleans preference alignment.
Abstract pp.1-2; Intro pp.2-3; Tab.1 p.7
Problem

Open JAVG was stuck between quality, synchrony, and system simplicity

  • Existing open-source JAVG lags Veo3-like proprietary systems in quality, temporal synchrony, and human preference alignment.
  • Unified-latent designs may erase modality-specific information; two-stream / intermediate-fusion designs add architectural complexity and inference cost.
  • Audio and video have incompatible native coordinates: video is temporal-height-width, audio is time-frequency.
  • Averaged preference scores can select a “winner” that improves video while worsening audio, which is a bad signal for joint generation.
The paper reframes JAVG as a coordination problem across architecture, position encoding, and preference data.
Abstract p.1; Related Work p.3; §3.1-3.4 pp.4-7
Design target

Do not stitch two models; extend a strong T2V backbone carefully

What they keep

  • Wan2.1-1.3B-T2V DiT backbone
  • Wan2.1 video VAE
  • AudioLDM2 audio VAE
  • umT5-xxl text encoder with 512 context length

What they add

  1. MS-MoE: modality-specific FFN experts after shared attention
  2. TA-RoPE: audio-video time alignment by position IDs
  3. AV-DPO: modality-aware preference alignment for rectified flows
§3.2 p.5; App. B.1 p.18
Contributions

Three moves, each attacking a different failure mode

  1. Unified but modality-aware DiT: concatenate tokens for full self-attention, then route audio/video tokens into separate FFNs.
  2. Temporally aligned RoPE: audio tokens share a video-time axis but avoid position-ID collisions in non-time axes.
  3. AV-DPO: select winner-loser pairs only when rewards improve consistently across modality-aware dimensions.
  4. Efficient recipe: about 1M public entries, 2-5s and 240p-480p support, final 2.1B model.
Intro p.3; §3.2-3.4 pp.4-7
Architecture

Shared attention exchanges information; modality-specific FFNs prevent interference

Fig.3 p.4; §3.2 pp.4-5
MS-MoE mechanism

Deterministic expert routing is enough because modality is known

Why not one FFN?

  • Shared-DiT + LoRA lacks trainable capacity for audio adaptation.
  • Shared-DiT + Full-FT shifts too many pretrained parameters and degrades video priors.
  • A single FFN forces audio and video tokens to fight for the same intra-modal modeling space.

Why MS-MoE?

  • Attention handles cross-modal exchange.
  • FFNs specialize after interaction, not before.
  • Total params grow 1.3B -> 2.1B, but activated params per token stay 1.3B.
This is MoE as architectural separation, not MoE as learned routing.
§3.2 p.5; Tab.2 p.8
TA-RoPE

Audio tokens borrow the video time axis, then move away in the other axes

Fig.4 p.5; §3.3 p.5
TA-RoPE formula

The formula encodes two constraints: shared time, non-overlap elsewhere

Ra(t,m) = (round(t·Tv/Ta), t + H, m + W)
  • round(t·Tv/Ta): aligns audio timestamp to the corresponding video temporal ID.
  • t + H, m + W: offsets the audio time-frequency plane away from video height-width IDs.
  • Full attention means they can emulate temporal interleaving with IDs; physical token reorder is unnecessary.
Synchrony is handled by geometry in position space, not by a separate slow alignment block.
Eq.(4) p.5; §3.3 p.5
AV-DPO pipeline

Preference data is built from multimodal reward dimensions, not a single scalar

Fig.5 p.6; §3.4 p.6
Preference construction

The subtle part: chosen pairs must be modality-consistent

  1. Collect a 30K prompt pool outside the SFT training data.
  2. Reference model generates N=3 audio-video candidates per prompt; ground-truth pairs are added as candidates.
  3. Reward models cover audio quality, video/motion quality, text-audio/text-video alignment, AV semantic alignment, and temporal synchrony.
  4. Keep a winner-loser pair only when modality-aware reward sums rank the winner higher across relevant dimensions.
30K promptsN=3 candidates~25K pairs~30% generated winners

Mechanism crown

The core insight is coordinate hygiene: share only the temporal axis, never let audio frequency coordinates collide with video spatial coordinates.

Appendix D.1 confirms the mechanism: fractional audio IDs hurt audio quality; overlapping IDs hurt video; missing temporal alignment hurts synchrony.

Scaling lesson

For JAVG, “more data” and “higher quality data” both fail alone

Ablation finding

  • 120K high-quality samples lack enough diversity for the hard audio-video transfer.
  • 720K low-quality samples degrade the strong Wan2.1 prior.
  • 330K medium-quality samples become the practical SFT trade-off.

Engineering implication

  • Do not blindly scale noisy sounding-video data.
  • Keep enough diversity before applying stronger quality thresholds.
  • Treat backbone prior preservation as a data-curation objective.
App. D.2 pp.23-24; Tab.A3 p.24
Limitations

The paper is strong, but not a general multimodal generator yet

  • Training data is around 1M entries, which may limit scaling compared with proprietary systems.
  • The backbone is still 1.3B with parameter-efficient adaptation; larger backbones may capture subtler semantic-temporal correlations.
  • Full-parameter finetuning is not deeply explored because cost rises.
  • Fine-grained controllability over music, speech, rhythm, pitch, timbre, or lexical content remains future work.
  • A2V, V2A, and audio-image-to-video are named as future directions rather than solved tasks.
App. A.1 p.17
For image / video engineers

What is reusable beyond this paper

  1. Separate interaction from specialization: use shared attention for mixing, then separate FFNs for modality-specific refinement.
  2. Encode alignment in coordinates when possible: a good positional scheme can replace a slow alignment module.
  3. Preference data must respect output structure: if output has multiple coupled modalities, one scalar reward can be misleading.
  4. Protect pretrained priors: low-quality multimodal data can damage a strong visual backbone faster than it teaches synchrony.
Synthesis from §3.2-3.4 and App. D.1-D.2
Appendix highlight · architecture

“we introduce a modality-specific mixture-of-experts (MS-MoE) design that enables cross-modal interaction efficacy while enhancing single-modal generation quality.”

作者把音訊與影片的互動交給共享 attention，把各自的細節建模交給 modality-specific FFN。Abstract p.1

“although the total parameter size increases from 1.3B to 2.1B, the number of activated parameters per token remains 1.3B.”

容量增加，但每個 token 實際啟用的參數量不變；這是 MS-MoE 的效率關鍵。§3.2 p.5

Appendix highlight · TA-RoPE

“absolute temporal alignment is enforced along the first dimension (dimension 0) of the 3D position IDs for both audio and video tokens.”

音訊與影片共享同一條時間軸，讓同步關係直接進入 RoPE 座標。§3.3 p.5

“we offset the original mel-spectrogram dimensions (Ta and M) by adding H and W, respectively.”

非時間座標被刻意錯開，避免音訊頻率座標和影片空間座標互相污染。§3.3 p.5

Appendix highlight · AV-DPO

“winning pairs from our generated samples occupy around 30% of the total preference data.”

參考模型本身已不弱，所以偏好資料不能只靠生成候選之間的微小差異；混入 ground truth 能穩定信號。§3.4 p.6

“applying modality-agnostic strategies like Average-Micro and Average-Macro fails to achieve consistent improvements.”

JAVG 的偏好排序如果只平均成單一分數，很容易選到跨 modality 互相矛盾的勝者。§4.3 p.9
