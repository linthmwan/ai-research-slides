Correct but
Unselectable

Multi-candidate reasoning has a third bottleneck: the interface between generation and selection.

NeurIPS 2026announced 2026-10-23
Qianli Ma et al. · Beijing Normal University / BNBU
Correct but Unselectable: The Hidden Interface Tax in Multi-Candidate Reasoning · Abstract / Figure 1, pp.1–2 · arXiv:2610.32235v1
TL;DR

A correct trace can already be in the pool — and still be unselectable.

CACC repairs the interface before a fixed selector, rather than retraining the generator or reranker.
Abstract p.1; §1 pp.2–3
WHY THIS MATTERS

Sampling more candidates is not a complete test-time scaling strategy

  • Small-model deployments are constrained by cost, latency, privacy, and controllability.
  • More samples can contain fragments, partial answers, wrong modes, or selector-attractive noise.
  • The pipeline has an unoptimized middle: candidate outputs must satisfy the selector and parser contracts.
Abstract p.1; §1 p.2
THE HIDDEN TAX

Two different reasons a final answer can fail

COVERAGE FAILURE

Correct answer absent

  • Generator never produced a usable correct trace.
  • More/better generation is the relevant intervention.

CONVERSION FAILURE

Correct answer present but unusable

  • Trace is incomplete, unparseable, wrong-mode, or drowned by noise.
  • Repair/alignment/hygiene is the relevant intervention.
§1 pp.2–3; §3 p.4
SYSTEM VIEW

CACC is a compatibility layer between a fixed generator and fixed selector

Figure 1 p.2
MEASUREMENT

Do not ask only “did a correct candidate appear?” Ask whether the fixed selector can consume it.

The key object is selectable coverage: Correct AND Parseable AND ModeMatch AND Clean.
§3 pp.4–5
COVERAGE x CONVERSION

A decomposition turns one opaque score into two actionable diagnoses

Afinal = Aoracle x V|O
  • A_oracle: any candidate in the retained pool parses to the gold answer.
  • V|O: probability that the selector is correct given an oracle-hit pool.
  • For an extractive selector, the identity is exact: low final accuracy can come from missing candidates or failed conversion.
Eq. 5–7 and §3 p.4
SELECTABLE COVERAGE

Raw correctness is insufficient for a real interface

RAW ORACLE HIT

  • A semantically correct candidate exists.
  • It may still fail a parser or be ignored by the reranker.

SELECTABLE ORACLE HIT

  • Correct
  • Parseable under benchmark mode
  • Mode-matched
  • Clean of selector-confusing residue
Selectable coverage is an operational diagnostic, not an assumed perfect upper bound.
Eq. 8–11, §3 pp.4–5
ARCHITECTURE

Offline-calibrated rules; online gold-free construction

Figure 2 / §4 pp.5–6
OFFLINE STAGE

Freeze the contract before evaluation

  1. Build the benchmark answer-mode registry and parser contract.
  2. Audit failure traces; define diagnostics for completion, mode, wrappers, residue, and conflicts.
  3. Instantiate CCR / AMC / SCH rule templates.
  4. Calibrate and freeze the routing policy on audit data.

Offline attribution may use audited/dev labels; online inference never reads test gold labels.

§4 pp.5–6; Algorithm 1 p.6
ONLINE STAGE

Apply a fixed policy to each raw candidate pool

  1. Infer answer mode m from the registry.
  2. Compute observable diagnostics: parser status, markers, residue, conflicts, scores.
  3. Repair / align / clean according to frozen rules.
  4. Deduplicate, parser-check, cap the pool, then call the unchanged selector.
The selector is held fixed: CACC changes the distribution arriving at its input.
§4 p.6; Algorithm 1 p.6
MODULE 1 / CCR

Completion-Centric Replacement repairs coverage failures

Trigger

  • Fragment or missing final-answer span.
  • Candidate slot falls below the parser completion threshold.

Action

  • Call completion-oriented generation.
  • Merge, rescore, and retain under the same cap.
  • Recover a parsable final answer from a partial derivation.
§4 p.6
MODULE 2 / AMC

Answer-Mode Compatibility repairs conversion failures

  • MCQ requires a benchmark-mandated choice letter; a numerically correct rationale can still fail readout.
  • Numeric tasks normalize the final numeric span while protecting boxed-answer wrappers.
  • Answer-mode registry and parser contract are frozen offline — not patched after seeing test labels.
Formatting is not cosmetic when the evaluator and selector are contract-bound.
§4 pp.5–6
MODULE 3 / SCH

Selector-Compatible Hygiene prevents correct answers from being drowned out

  • Detect residue, scaffold leakage, wrapper drift, conflicts, and selector-attractive distractors.
  • Keep original and constructed candidates, then deduplicate last.
  • The retained cap should contain mode-aligned, diverse candidates — not repeated variants of one scaffold.

COUNTEREXAMPLE

Coverage expansion at the wrong layer can backfire

  • On GSM8K, SP -> Power Sampling x CACC raises oracle by +10.84 pp.
  • But V|O falls by -12.43 pp, and final accuracy falls by -2.50 pp.
  • The paper’s prescription: choose interventions by the bottleneck layer, not by the number of stacked enhancements.

§5 p.8; Table 3 p.9
ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

Run an interface audit before paying for more inference

  1. Measure raw coverage, conversion V|O, and final accuracy separately.
  2. Inspect parser contracts, answer modes, final markers, and selector-attractive residue.
  3. Repair the layer that owns the failure: source, interface, or selector.
  4. Keep compute-regime changes explicit; do not fold stronger proposers into matched CACC claims.
§5–6 pp.8–10
LIMITS

The method is a useful diagnosis, not a universal free gain

  • Current registry targets numeric and MCQ parser contracts; free-form, code, and multi-turn interfaces remain open.
  • The rulebook is manually calibrated per benchmark; learned routing from selector feedback could reduce setup cost.
  • Effects depend on selector, readout, and scope — the original MMLU-Pro decline is the paper’s own warning.
  • CCR may call the generator, so total inference cost is not matched even with a fixed retained cap.
§4 p.6; §6 p.10

APPENDIX / THE CENTRAL CLAIM

Layer matching (not the number of stacked enhancements) is what predicts whether a given composition converts oracle expansion into final accuracy under a fixed selector.

決定 oracle 擴張能否變成最終準確率的，是介入層位是否匹配，而不是疊了多少強化模組。
