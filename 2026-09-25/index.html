Baseline Shape Decides the Verdict: A Controlled Re-Examination of Ternary Language Models at 60K Parameters · arXiv:2609.29397v1 · p.1
TL;DR

At 60K parameters, baseline shape can decide the architecture verdict before the architecture does.

A reported routed-ternary gain vanishes against the best short-budget transformer shape; at longer budget recurrent systems win, but routing is not required.
Abstract p.1; §6.1–6.2 pp.5–6
WHY THIS MATTERS

Ternary is compelling at MCU scale — evidence is fragile.

  • Ternary weights use 1.58 bits and can reduce FP32 storage by up to roughly 20x.
  • The target regime is tens to hundreds of KB of flash/SRAM, not billion-parameter scaling.
  • A prominent 60K comparison claimed a routed ternary model beat a parameter-matched FP transformer by 22%.

CONTRIBUTION

A modest study, designed to make the verdict falsifiable.

  1. Audit the released Atome-LM artifacts instead of treating headline points as commensurate.
  2. Control recipe, byte budget, parameter count, seeds, and the FP anchor.
  3. Sweep five transformer shapes plus routed and gated-SSM families.
  4. Expose two choices — baseline shape and stage-2 LR — that each reverse a headline.

REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

The routed block has three parallel paths, then a per-token softmax mix.

  • Depthwise causal convolution (k=5)
  • Diagonal SSM
  • Single-head top-k causal attention
  • No feed-forward layer
  • Router supplies channel mixing
  • Residual after mixed paths
h_t = a * h_(t-1) + b * x_t; y_t = c * h_t
The control comparison asks whether this router adds value beyond a simpler recurrent alternative.

DATA + MEASUREMENT

Hold the recipe fixed; make “real” mean larger than seed noise.

  • TinyStories V2 raw bytes; 2.03B training tokens after a 10% held-out split.
  • 16M and 130M bytes are only 0.008 and 0.064 epochs — repeated-data memorization is avoided.
  • Primary metric: final-checkpoint validation loss in nats/byte on 256 held-out batches.
  • A relative gap counts as real only when it exceeds the pre-specified 2-sigma seed-noise threshold.

§6.1 p.5
BUDGET REVERSAL

The best transformer shape must be swept per budget.

  • At 16M bytes: 1x52 is best among the transformer shapes (2.835 FP32 nats/byte).
  • At 130M bytes: 1x52 becomes worst (2.195), while deeper 3x32 becomes best (2.143).
  • The paper defines “shape” as the complete depth/width/FFN/position-budget package, not depth alone.
Choose a baseline once and reuse it at another budget: that is exactly the mistake this study catches.
§6.1 p.6; Table 4 p.6
LONGER-BUDGET VERDICT

At 130M bytes, recurrent architectures really do pull ahead — but routing is not necessary.

  • Routed beats each of the three re-run transformer shapes by 22.2–24.0% in FP32 and 13.7–14.4% in ternary, outside noise.
  • Gated SSM beats routed by 9.1% in FP32 (2-sigma relative noise: 3.2%).
  • Gated SSM vs routed in ternary is -2.6%, inside its 3.7% noise band.

TERNARY PENALTY

The penalty differs sharply at 130M — but the causal story is unfinished.

+5.3% transformer+19.5% routed+28.1% gated SSM
  • Transformer penalty falls from +20.9% to +5.3%.
  • Recurrent penalties do not shrink at the measured budgets.
  • This is an observation, not an architecture law.
§6.3 p.7; Table 4 p.6
CAUSAL CAVEATS

Three live alternatives prevent an architecture-only conclusion.

  1. Unequal quantization: transformer positional embeddings remain FP32.
  2. Untuned ternary baseline: from-scratch ternary uses the FP peak LR.
  3. Loss level covaries: recurrence and transformer families reach different absolute losses at 130M.
The defensible claim is “observed penalty differences,” not “architecture causes the difference.”

OPTIMIZATION INTERPRETATION

The ternary stage may be re-solving weights, not merely fine-tuning them.

Loss continues falling through a stage-2 LR of 3e-3 — about 10x the FP pretraining peak — and the sweep has not saturated. Treat 3e-3 as a lower bound, not an optimum.

LIMITS

This is not a general scaling law for ternary models.

  • One parameter count: 60K; no answer for 1M, 10M, or the original 944K reversal.
  • One byte-level corpus: TinyStories may favor local sequential structure and recurrent/convolutional paths.
  • Only two budgets; short budget is 245 optimizer steps.
  • No MCU deployment metric; no per-shape LR tuning; gated SSM is not a Mamba-class baseline.
§7 pp.8–9
ENGINEERING TAKEAWAYS

What changes in a compression experiment tomorrow?

  1. Sweep parameter-matched shapes at every compute/data budget; do not canonize one baseline.
  2. Report the exact fraction left FP32, not only nominal weight bit-width.
  3. Tune the quantized-stage optimizer separately; “FP-init” is not a complete recipe.
  4. Pre-register seed/noise criteria and publish configurations/logs so the comparison can be audited.
Synthesis grounded in §5–7 pp.4–9
