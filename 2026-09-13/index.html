OmniVoice

從 diffusion language model 到 600+ languages 的 zero-shot TTS

Han Zhu et al. - Xiaomi Corp. - arXiv:2604.00688v3
Preprint - under review2026-04-21 v3

GitHub Trending selection: k2-fsa/OmniVoice (2026-09-13). Paper announcement is backfilled 145 days; it is not presented as a conference acceptance.

Source: paper p.1-2; GitHub README
TL;DR

把 text 直接補成 multi-codebook acoustic tokens；真正讓單階段 NAR 撐住的，是 dense masking + LLM linguistic prior。

不走 text-to-semantic-to-acoustic cascade，目標同時解決 error propagation、information bottleneck 與語言覆蓋。
Abstract p.1; §1 p.2
PROBLEM

擴語言數，不只是把 corpus 變大

  • 多語 zero-shot TTS 多半只涵蓋數十種語言；低資源語言會先被排除。
  • Discrete NAR 的兩階段 cascade 會把 semantic prediction 的錯誤傳到 acoustic output。
  • 低 bitrate semantic representation 也會壓掉細緻 acoustic detail。
  • 以前 direct single-stage 雖簡潔，intelligibility 往往輸給 cascade。
§1 p.1
METHOD - ARCHITECTURE

一個 bidirectional Transformer，直接完成 acoustic reconstruction

Architecture bet: remove the semantic middle layer, then make the one-stage token model strong enough to carry both language and acoustics.
Fig. 1 p.2; §2.1 p.3
METHOD - OBJECTIVE

只有 masked acoustic positions 進 loss

L = - SUM_(t,c in M) log P(xt,c | X, Y; params)

X is a T x C multi-codebook acoustic matrix; Y concatenates instruction and transcript text tokens.

  • Prompt segment provides voice context.
  • Target segment mixes visible and [M] token positions.
  • C codebook-specific heads reconstruct acoustic token distributions.
§2.1, Eq. 1 p.3
METHOD - DENSE MASKING

不只選一層：每個 codebook entry 都有機會被 mask

  • For each sample, pt is sampled from U(0,1).
  • Every T x C entry is independently masked with Bernoulli(pt).
  • Paper claim: about 50% of tokens contribute to loss on average - C times the per-layer strategy.
§2.1.1, Fig. 2 p.3
METHOD - LLM INITIALIZATION

把 AR LLM 的語言知識移植給 bidirectional NAR TTS

Transfer

  • Backbone is structurally compatible with a standard AR LLM.
  • Initialize from Qwen3-0.6B weights.
  • Then operate with bidirectional attention for masked acoustic recovery.

Hypothesis

  • Pretrained linguistic structure acts as a text-to-speech prior.
  • It targets the intelligibility gap previously seen in direct single-stage discrete NAR models.
§2.1.2 p.4; §3.2 p.6
SCALING

581k hours / 600+ languages: data curation and resampling are part of the method

  • Aggregate 50 open-source datasets; restore degraded speech and filter invalid transcripts.
  • Use language-level repetition factors to counter the long tail; beta=0.8 balances high-resource retention and low-resource exposure.
  • Use pretrained-LLM subword tokenization rather than language-specific G2P and text normalization.
581k hours600+ languages50 datasetsbeta=0.8
§2.2 pp.4-5; Fig. 3 p.4
CONTROLLABILITY

同一個 backbone 把「像誰、乾不乾淨、怎麼念」拆成三種控制

  1. Acoustic: train noisy/reverberant prompts with a denoise instruction, separating identity from environment.
  2. Identity: speaker attributes such as gender, age, pitch, accent/dialect become instruction tokens for voice design.
  3. Linguistic: paralinguistic cues plus Pinyin / CMU phoneme overrides give explicit pronunciation control.
§2.3 p.5
TRAINING + INFERENCE

0.8B model, 8 codebooks, iterative unmasking

Training

  • Qwen3-0.6B initialized bidirectional Transformer.
  • Higgs-audio tokenizer supplies 8-codebook acoustic tokens.
  • 8 H800 GPUs; multilingual run: 2M updates in 9.66 days.

Generation

  • 32 iterative unmasking steps.
  • Temperature 5 for position sampling; argmax token assignment.
  • Layer penalty and classifier-free guidance scale 2.
§3.2-3.4 p.6
EVALUATION

從 bilingual voice cloning 到 102-language stress test

  • LibriSpeech-PC and Seed-TTS test standard English / Chinese zero-shot settings.
  • MiniMax-Multilingual-24 compares 24 languages against commercial systems.
  • FLEURS-Multilingual-102 is a constructed 102-language benchmark.
  • Metrics: SIM-o, WER/CER, UTMOS plus CMOS and SMOS human studies.

HOW TO READ THE CLAIM

What is well supported, and what stays provisional

Supported by reported evidence

  • Dense masking beats reported masking baselines in a controlled Emilia ablation.
  • LLM initialization improves WER across three evaluated datasets.
  • Breadth is demonstrated on 24- and 102-language evaluations.

Still not settled

  • The paper remains under review, not accepted.
  • No public reviewer record was acquired.
  • ASR-based CER can saturate; audio quality cannot be inferred from one automatic metric.
Front matter p.1; §4.2-4.3 pp.7-11
ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

Two targeted fixes can make a simpler generative pipeline competitive

  1. Choose the bottleneck: remove an intermediate semantic stage only when the direct model has enough token-level supervision.
  2. Spend compute densely: full-codebook masking improves the amount and coverage of supervised signal per update.
  3. Reuse priors deliberately: AR LLM initialization is not incompatible with a bidirectional NAR downstream objective.
  4. Audit metrics: language breadth needs per-language diagnostics, and ASR scores are not the same thing as perceived speech quality.
§2.1-2.2 pp.3-5; §4.2-4.3 pp.7-11
